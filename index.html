17. Two grids: the same person, twice

1 item
📝 Organic, not an ad. Threads, vlds.lvr, 8K likes, 403 comments, 75 reposts, caption "Наглядный пример как выглядит депрессия". Two monthly grids of activity rings side by side, March against July. No text, no explanation, no person: the state is read off a calendar in one second and the reader supplies the story. For us the two grids are two antidepressants rather than two months, which is the one format where multimedication shows without a single product screen. The claim has to sit on the record, not on the mood.

18. Casting call static: we are looking for people who…

2 items
📝 Stories statics, 9:16, all caps, centred, no person and no product screen. The build is fixed: WE ARE LOOKING FOR, then who exactly, then the state they are in, then AND WANT TO TRY, then the mechanism underlined, then one line of payoff, then a tap instruction. It reads as a recruitment notice rather than an ad, which is why the reader checks whether it is about them. The background is decorative and unrelated to the subject, scribbles on cream in 18-1, paper stars on pastel in 18-2, and it exists only to keep the block of text from looking like a wall.
